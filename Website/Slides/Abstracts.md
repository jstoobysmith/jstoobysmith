## Abstracts for Talks

Title: Physlib: Digitalizing physics into Lean 4

Abstract: The advent of AI has changed many aspects of our lives, including how we work. Within the world of mathematics one way to harness the power of AI is to use a programming language called Lean. In Lean one can write mathematical definitions, theorems and their proofs in a way that mathematical consitency and correctness is gaurnteed. Feedback loops can be set up in which AI write mathematics in Lean, Lean gives feedback on the correctness, and the AI can then use this feedback to improve. Mathematics in Lean has been driven by a large community project called Mathlib. This talk is about an effort to create a corresponding library for physics, called Physlib. I will summarize what Lean is, what using it in physics gives you, and what the current state of Physlib.
